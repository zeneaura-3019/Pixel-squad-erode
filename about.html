About PixelSquad

We Turn Ideas Into Visual Impact.

PixelSquad combines creative visuals, photography, videography and digital marketing under one studio — built to help brands grow with work that actually looks and performs like it belongs to them. We don't hand off a folder of assets and disappear; we build the visual language a brand keeps using for years.

Founder of PixelSquad

Ranjithpriyan S S S

Ranjithpriyan built PixelSquad on the floor of the cine industry — years spent on real film sets shaped an eye for light, pacing and story that most agencies never develop. That craft now drives every shoot, edit and campaign PixelSquad puts out, backed by a working knowledge of the platforms and strategy that make creative actually perform.
